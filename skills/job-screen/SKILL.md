---
name: job-screen
description: Screen a job posting against the candidate's explicit criteria and career evidence; return pursue, hold, or skip with traceable reasons.
---

Read the posting snapshot/raw description, search preferences, rubric and relevant profile/career evidence. For a supplied posting without intake, follow [intake](../job-discover/SKILL.md). If the rubric is missing, provide a provisional qualitative assessment and ask for criteria before numeric or automatic selection.

Separate actual role duties and hard eligibility from preferences. Check scope, seniority, location/travel, work arrangement, qualifications and application language when relevant. Distinguish a genuine incompatibility from missing information. Report evidence as direct, adjacent, unsupported or unknown. Quote exact requirements and cite candidate evidence IDs, with notes on contradictory sources. Avoid rewarding title overlap alone. Apply the candidate’s weights and thresholds consistently and retain the score even when the user gives a job special attention.

Write the screening JSON in [the contract](../../docs/WORKFLOW.md) and explain why to pursue, hold or skip. Surface unresolved required facts and strongest matches; distinguish unavailable sources from known gaps. Reassess when posting, rubric or user decisions change. Do not change canonical candidate facts or authorize application.
