# Career interview question bank

Use these as a menu, not a script. Adapt the wording to the person and the role.

## Context

- What did the organization do, and what stage was it at when you joined (size, revenue, funding, market position)?
- Why did you join? What were you hired to do, and how was success defined?
- Who did you report to? Who were your peers?
- How big was your scope: direct and indirect reports, budget or P&L, products, regions, customers?
- What was the situation when you arrived? What was working and what was broken?

## Accomplishments

- What are you most proud of from this role?
- What was the situation, and why did it matter to the business?
- What did you personally do? What did your team do?
- What changed as a result? How was it measured, against what baseline and over what period?
- Who else would confirm this result?
- Can that number or client name be used publicly, or should we describe it differently?

## Leadership (especially for senior and executive tracks)

- How did you build or reshape the team? How many hires, and what did you change in how the team worked?
- Tell me about a hard people decision you made.
- How did you work with the CEO, the board, investors or other executives?
- What strategy did you set, and how did you get the organization behind it?
- What did you transform: a process, a culture, a business model, a platform?
- What commercial impact did your work have: revenue, margin, cost, growth, retention?

## Low points

- What were the biggest mistakes or failures in this role?
- What would you do differently with hindsight?
- What was the hardest moment, and how did you handle it?

## How others see you

- If I called your manager from this role, what would they say your biggest strengths were?
- And what would they say you needed to improve?
- How would your team describe your leadership?

## Transitions

- Why did you leave? Whose decision was it?
- What did you look for in the next role, and did you find it?

## Across the whole career (for `career-story.md`)

- What thread connects your roles?
- What do people consistently come to you for?
- What do you want next, and what do you never want to do again?
- Are there gaps, short tenures or pivots that you'll need to explain? How do you explain them?
