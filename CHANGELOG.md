# What's new

Each version lists what changed for people using the toolkit. To get the latest version, see "Staying up to date" in the [README](README.md#staying-up-to-date).

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-16)

- **CV templates.** Your CV PDF now comes in three styles (editorial, gutter and banner), with six accent colours or one of your own. All three stay easy for applicant tracking systems to read.
- **Autopilot.** A new daily run looks for new roles across your saved sources, screens them and reports what's worth a look.
- **Bring your own CV.** You can ask for a review of a CV you wrote yourself, and attach your own documents to an application.
- **Smoother setup.** Clearer hints when PDF tools are missing (including the Xcode licence on a Mac), better handling of hidden folders, and a welcome that separates what the toolkit can do from what it promises.
- **Easier to find review and submission.** Setup now tells you how to get a CV reviewed and how applications are handled.
- **Fixes from beta testers.** More reliable PDF export checks, correct paper size, Word import, saved screenings that stay current, and bold text that no longer breaks across lines.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-13)

- First beta: guided setup, career interview, learning loop, and packaging as a plugin for Claude and Codex.
